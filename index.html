The Covenant

From first letter to full custody in four nights.

  1. Invitation

    You write to us. A keeper replies within three nights and comes only when invited in.

  2. Survey by candlelight

    We walk every hall and vault, then count what is owned, what is owed and what is buried.

  3. The signing

    Terms are set down in plain language and signed in ink. Only ink, since the reforms of 1702.

    Your keeper takes the keys. A sealed ledger arrives each quarter at dusk, whether you are awake or not.
